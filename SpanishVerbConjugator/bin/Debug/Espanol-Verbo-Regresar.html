regresar - to return

regresar: to return
Regular first-conjugation Spanish verb
Transitive verb (takes a direct object)
Intransitive verb (does not take a direct object)

infinitive: regresar
gerund: regresando
present participle: regresando
past participle: regresado
auxiliary verb: haber

El Presente Perfecto - The Present Perfect Tense

The perfect tense in Spanish has two parts to it. The present tense of the verb haber (to have) plus the past participle. The present prefect tense expresses past action closely related to the present. The English equivalent of haber + past participle is to have done something.

This is the present perfect indicative, not the present perfect subjunctive.

El Imperfecto de Indicativo - The Imperfect Tense
Español Inglés Español Inglés
yo regresaba I was returning, I used to return nosotros regresábamos we were returning, we used to return
tú regresabas you were returning, you used to return vosotros regresabais you were returning, you used to return
usted regresaba you were returning, you used to return ustedes regresaban you were returning, you used to return
él regresaba he was returning, he used to return ellos regresaban they were returning, they used to return
ella regresaba she was returning, she used to return ellas regresaban they were returning, they used to return

The imperfect tense expresses actions in the past that are not seen as completed. It is used to indicate situations or actions in the past with no specific reference to their beginning or end.

The imperfect indicative tense can be translated to English as "used to return" or "was returning."

Was: is used with singular pronouns like "I" and "he".
Were: is used with plural pronouns like "we" and "they".
However, were is also used for "you".
