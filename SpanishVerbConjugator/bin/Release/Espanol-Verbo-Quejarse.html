quejarse - to complain

quejarse: to complain
Regular first-conjugation Spanish verb
Transitive verb (takes a direct object)
Intransitive verb (does not take a direct object)

infinitive: quejarse
gerund: quejando
present participle: quejando
past participle: quejado
auxiliary verb: haber

El Presente Perfecto - The Present Perfect Tense
Español Inglés Español Inglés
yo me he quejado I have complained nosotros nos hemos quejado we have complained
tú te has quejado you have complained vosotros vos habéis quejado you have complained
usted se ha quejado you have complained ustedes se han quejado you have complained
él se ha quejado he has complained ellos se han quejado they have complained
ella se ha quejado she has complained ellas se han quejado they have complained

The perfect tense in Spanish has two parts to it. The present tense of the verb haber (to have) plus the past participle. The present prefect tense expresses past action closely related to the present. The English equivalent of haber + past participle is to have done something.

This is the present perfect indicative, not the present perfect subjunctive.

El Imperfecto de Indicativo - The Imperfect Tense
Español Inglés Español Inglés
yo me quejaba I was complaining, I used to complain nosotros nos quejábamos we were complaining, we used to complain
tú te quejabas you were complaining, you used to complain vosotros vos quejabais you were complaining, you used to complain
usted se quejaba you were complaining, you used to complain ustedes se quejaban you were complaining, you used to complain
él se quejaba he was complaining, he used to complain ellos se quejaban they were complaining, they used to complain
ella se quejaba she was complaining, she used to complain ellas se quejaban they were complaining, they used to complain

The imperfect tense expresses actions in the past that are not seen as completed. It is used to indicate situations or actions in the past with no specific reference to their beginning or end.

The imperfect indicative tense can be translated to English as "used to complain" or "was complaining."

Was: is used with singular pronouns like "I" and "he".
Were: is used with plural pronouns like "we" and "they".
However, were is also used for "you".
